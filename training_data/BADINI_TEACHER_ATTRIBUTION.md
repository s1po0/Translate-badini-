# Badini teacher examples

`badini_model_card_examples.csv` contains the two English-to-Badini example
outputs published in the model card of [arkanit/badini-nllb-translator](https://huggingface.co/arkanit/badini-nllb-translator),
by Arkan Musa Zubair. These examples were transcribed from the card, not
produced by running the model in this environment. They are a very small set,
not evidence of improved model quality, and have not been independently
validated. The model card labels the derivative MIT, but it identifies its base
as NLLB-200 distilled 600M (CC BY-NC 4.0); the combined model/data provenance
and rights need review before commercial use or redistribution.
